How to use the LL(1) module

1. Enter your grammar and click Load Grammar.

2. In the First & Follow tab, click Next Step to add one derivation at a time, or Compute All to compute everything at once.

3. In the Parse Table tab, click Next Step to fill one cell at a time. Conflicted cells are highlighted in red.

4. In the String Parsing tab, enter a test string and click Next Step to execute one stack move at a time until ACCEPT or REJECT.

Note: The grammar must be free of left recursion and not ambiguous for LL(1). Conflicts are detected and flagged automatically.

Grammar format: S -> A B | ε — one rule per line, | separates alternatives.
